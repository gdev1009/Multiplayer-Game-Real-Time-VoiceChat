Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the App Store or Google Play → Subscriptions → Match Word → Cancel. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.

How do I delete my account?
Email support@grandmamac.com from the address linked to your account and ask for account deletion.

I can't hear other players
Check microphone permissions in device Settings → Match Word. Ensure you are not muted in the game room.

How do I join a friend's game?
Tap Join by Code on the home screen and enter the four-digit room code your friend shared.
