Match Word — Privacy Policy

Grandma Mac · Last updated: July 14, 2026

Match Word (“the App”) is operated by Grandma Mac (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account data: email address, display name, and profile information you provide when you sign up.
  • Gameplay data: game rooms, scores, character customization choices, friends list, and in-app activity needed to run multiplayer games.
  • Device data: device identifiers used for sign-in, crash logs, and basic analytics to keep the App stable.
  • Voice (optional): if you use voice chat, audio is transmitted in real time for gameplay and is not stored by us for marketing purposes.
  • Purchases: subscription status is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play; we receive entitlement status, not full payment card numbers.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and sync your account across devices.
  • Match you with other players and run live game sessions.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • Improve reliability, security, and features of the App.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

We use trusted third parties to operate the App, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend hosting.
  • Apple / Google — app distribution and in-app subscriptions.
  • Email delivery — transactional messages (e.g. sign-in links).

These providers process data only as needed to deliver their services to us.

Data retention

We keep account and gameplay data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see Support).

Children

Match Word is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls. See our Security Policy for more detail.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
